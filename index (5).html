A little background

Where biology meets data.

I am a doctoral researcher in Ohio State’s Interdisciplinary PhD Program in Nutrition and a Graduate Research Associate in the Department of Human Sciences. In the Wilson Lab, my research connects dietary bioactives and the gut microbiome with host metabolic health. My background spans nutritional sciences and human biology, lipid metabolomics, experimental models, and molecular research. I am also interested in using machine learning and integrated omics data to understand variation in responses to diet and physiological stress.
